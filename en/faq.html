Frequently asked questions

ShoeTracker is a free mileage tracker for running shoes. The most common questions about durability, offline use and privacy – answered briefly.

How many miles does a running shoe last?

As a rule of thumb, 375 to 560 miles (600 to 900 kilometres). The actual lifespan depends on body weight, running style, surface and cushioning material. That is why ShoeTracker lets you set the limit per shoe and starts with a default of 500 miles or 800 kilometres.

The app marks a shoe as “Due soon” from 75 percent of its set mileage and as “Worn out” from 90 percent – early enough to get a replacement before your knees and shins send the bill.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. ShoeTracker stores shoes and runs exclusively in your browser’s local storage. There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud and no analytics or advertising scripts. The app only loads its own files.

Which keys are used and how to delete them again is explained in the privacy policy.

Does ShoeTracker work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the app is kept in the browser cache and can be installed on your home screen as a Progressive Web App. From then on it starts without an internet connection.

How do I back up my data or switch devices?

In the Statistics section, “Data & backup” offers an export as a JSON file. You can import this file again on another device or in another browser. There is also a CSV export for Excel.

Since there is no server, this backup is your only protection against data loss: clearing the browser’s site data also clears your entries.

Why is my data gone after closing the browser?

Some browsers automatically delete all site data when they close, for example Brave, or Chrome when the corresponding privacy setting is on. That also removes your shoes and runs.

The fix is an exception for this site: in your browser settings under Privacy and security → Cookies and site data, add weekendcompiler.github.io to the sites that are always allowed to use cookies. If you clear browser data by hand, you clear the entries too – only a backup protects against that.

What does ShoeTracker cost?

Nothing. The app is free, ad-free and works without a user account.

What else can the app do?

If you rotate several pairs, you can drag them by their handle into the order you want and move retired shoes to the archive without losing their history. Runs are logged with date and a note about the route, and the statistics show useful summaries. Distances can be shown in kilometres or miles. As an installable Progressive Web App, ShoeTracker runs on Android, iOS, Windows, macOS and Linux.
