Frequently asked questions

ShoeTracker is a free mileage tracker for running shoes. The most common questions about durability, offline use and privacy - answered briefly.

How many miles does a running shoe last?

As a rule of thumb, around 375 to 620 miles. The actual lifespan depends on the model, running style, body weight, surface and the cushioning materials used. In addition, shoes can age independently of their usage due to UV light and oxidation. As a default, ShoeTracker assumes 500 miles of mileage. Since technologies and materials are constantly changing and the wear of e.g. trail running shoes and shoes with carbon plates can behave completely differently from each other, this value can be set individually for each shoe model.

What wear zones does ShoeTracker use?

The app divides the condition of running shoes into four zones. Newly purchased shoes have "out of the box" a very poppy running feel with lots of "bounce". That is why ShoeTracker marks shoes up to 25 percent of the set mileage as Fresh. After a while they feel a bit less lively and new, but are still perfectly usable for a long time. They are considered Good up to 75 percent. After that they have left the sweet spot of health protection, running comfort and performance. From 75 percent, the shoes are Due soon and you should start thinking about getting new ones. If a shoe exceeds 90 percent of its mileage, it is considered Worn out. At the latest, you should get a replacement now so that you still have some buffer until you completely retire them and file them in the archive. These default values for the wear zone thresholds can be configured freely.

What else can the app do?

If you run several running shoes in rotation, you can drag them by their handle into any order you want or have them automatically sorted by condition or remaining mileage. Retired shoes can be archived without losing their history. Runs can be logged with date and notes about the route, and the statistics show useful evaluations. As an installable Progressive Web App, ShoeTracker runs on Android, iOS, Windows, macOS and Linux.

Is my data sent to a server or sold?

No. ShoeTracker stores shoes and runs exclusively in your browser's local storage. There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud and no analytics or advertising scripts. The app sends nothing and only loads its own files.

More details can be found in the privacy policy.

Does ShoeTracker work offline?

Yes. After the first call, the app is stored in your browser's cache and can be installed on your home screen as a Progressive Web App. From then on it starts without an internet connection.

How do I back up my data or switch devices?

In the Statistics section, under Data & backup, there is an export as a JSON file. This file can be played back on another device or in another browser. Additionally, there is a CSV export for Excel. Since there is no server, this manual backup is the only protection against data loss!

Why is my data gone?

Some browsers automatically delete all site data when they close when the corresponding privacy setting is activated. This also removes all your shoes and runs.

To prevent this, manually create an exception for the ShoeTracker page. Usually in the browser settings under Privacy or Security → Cookies and site data. Enter the address "weekendcompiler.github.io" here in the list of websites that are always allowed to use cookies. If you clear browser data manually, you also clear the entries - only a previous backup protects against this.

What does ShoeTracker cost?

Nothing. The app is free and without any adds.
